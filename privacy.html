Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

My Medical Catalogue ("the App") is a shared catalogue of images and videos. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Google account: name, email address and profile photo, when you sign in with Google.
  • Content you post: images, titles, descriptions, comments and likes, shown with your display name.
  • Camera and gallery: accessed only when you tap Camera or Gallery to choose a photo to post. We do not access them in the background.
  • Location (GPS): only if you tap "Add location" on a post. It is optional and stored with that post.
  • Local storage: your appearance settings and access state are stored on your device.

How we use it

To authenticate you, display your posts and comments, let administrators moderate content, and keep the App secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third parties

Data is processed by Supabase (authentication, database, storage), Google (sign-in), Netlify (hosting) and YouTube (embedded videos, loaded in privacy-enhanced mode).

Patient privacy

Do not upload images that identify patients unless you have their written consent and the right to share them.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and content at any time by emailing YOUR-EMAIL@example.com. You can revoke camera or location permission in your browser or device settings.
